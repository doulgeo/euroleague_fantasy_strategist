What do these columns mean?
Drop
A player on this manager's roster who's being suggested as the weaker side of a same-position upgrade.
Position
Shared position for the swap - suggestions only ever compare players at the same position (Guard/Forward/Center).
Current proj.
The rostered player's projected PIR (fantasy scoring stat) for their next game.
Add
A free agent (not owned by any manager) at the same position, projected higher.
Available proj.
That free agent's projected PIR for their next game.
Gain
Available proj. minus Current proj. - the projected point gain from making this swap. Only shown above a minimum threshold, so small/noisy gains aren't suggested.
