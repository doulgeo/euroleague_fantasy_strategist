Fantasy draft pool

The real EuroLeague Fantasy game's actual draftable pool, from a user-maintained Google Sheet (not the EuroLeague API) - this is the primary source of roster composition (who's on which team, at what position) for the draft board and transfer suggestions, found to be more complete and current than EuroLeague's own roster data especially pre-season. If this hasn't been synced yet, EuroLeague's own roster data is used instead.
