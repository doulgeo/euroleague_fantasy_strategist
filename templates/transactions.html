What do these columns mean?
When
Timestamp this transaction was logged in the tool - not necessarily when it happened in the real league.
Round
Fantasy round number this transaction applies to, if you entered one.
Type
How the player changed hands: a draft pick, a free-agent add, a drop, or a trade.
Player
The player involved.
From / To
Manager who lost / gained the player. Blank "From" means a draft pick or free-agent add (no prior owner); blank "To" means a drop (no new owner).
Group
Shared ID linking the two legs of a trade - both players in a 1-for-1 trade show the same group ID.
Notes
Free-text notes you entered when logging the transaction.
