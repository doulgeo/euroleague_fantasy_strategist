What do these columns mean?
#
Rank within this position, best projected value first.
Player
Player name. A NEW badge means no EuroLeague box-score history in this project's local data (E2023 onward) - a new signing/rookie, or a club new to the league. Their projection is a placeholder (0.0) since there's no data yet to base one on.
Team
Current club, from the most recently synced roster (run sync_rosters.py to refresh).
Proj+Bonus
Projected PIR (the fantasy scoring stat) for this player's next game: a recency-weighted average of their last 10 games, plus a bonus scaled to their team's recent win rate.
VORP
Value over replacement: how much better this player projects than the last player at the same position who'd plausibly still get drafted across all 12 managers. Makes positions comparable - a scarce Center can rank above a slightly-higher-projected Guard once scarcity is factored in.
n
Games sampled to build the projection (up to 10, most recent weighted highest). Fewer than 3 games means no real projection can be built yet.
Owner
Manager who has already drafted this player, if any - drafted rows are greyed out.
— tier break —
A divider marking a bigger-than-usual drop-off to the next player at this position - players above the line are roughly interchangeable, so don't reach for one over another within the same tier.
